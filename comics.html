Title: “Trois et le chien” Narrative comic project

A narrative comic project exploring themes of loss, friendship, and hope. The story follows Oritte and Enyuk as they search for a missing dog, moving through quiet moments and emotional encounters. The project focuses on atmosphere, pacing, and visual storytelling.
